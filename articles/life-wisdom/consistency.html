Keep showing up

This is a reminder for me. I want you to direct attention on what you know you gotta do; it's fine if sometimes you wonder "hum. what if this is not it?" but let it go. Stay true to yourself and keep up your promises, no matter if you're too tired or if you think you've done "enough". The mind is trick - and there's something greater that rules it; you're not your tired body, you're not your fcked up brain, not your goals and definitely you're not your mind - all of these are executers and you're just awareness. Detach from the outcome and stay grinding on the process. Build trust in yourself, and when the odds are stacked against you, know that you have been consistent and are fulfilling your purpose.

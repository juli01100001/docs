build your identity and achieve your goals

this involves a lotta factors, but in the end it all comes down to identity. once you tune into your true self (which can be anything you set yourself to, no matter how hard it seems), you’ll see yourself accomplishing things that would’ve seemed impossible months, years, or even weeks/days ago. i never thought i could be so badass to the point of setting a project, doing it, and actually working on it. i don’t think about finishing it - that’s the coolest part. i just wanna make it better, and when i put my ideas to work and merge them with what i already have, it all becomes so satisfying.

but let me be honest: it wasn’t easy to get here. it’s always been simple, but never easy. and it would’ve been easier if i had built my identity in the first place instead of obsessing over my goals. goals are awesome, but you’ll never achieve them if you’re not aligned with your identity - the persona that could reach those milestones you’ve always dreamed of. so, just let this sit. this is enough. you don’t need anything else to change yourself. read it again - there’s no tutorial. but maybe i’ll make one someday who knows.

There's nothing wrong

There’s no problem until you decide there is As you evolve you can start to exercise a new world perspective. Depeding on how you’ve grown it’s going to be more difficult to cultivate a new form of being - but not so much if you really want it and expose yourself to experiences that torce you to get used to new outcomes. I say that because if there’s nothing wrong then it’s everything alright ( you can dig deeper on this and label it - alchemy - but there’s no need to) and if there’s nothing wrong you’re kinda free to do whatever you want to your life and be very successful at it.

Example If you went through hardship and now you look at it like damn, this really helped me to develop a sense of how x,y,z is important now, and im definitely grateful for that because now I’m a better person than I was.

then, no matter what happened to you, you overcome with gratitude and show to the universe you’re humble and open to new experiences,because there’s no failure and only success - if something went positive, amazing; if anything went negative, awesome - thus you overcome polarity, which makes you special because then you can manage to navigate any situation in life, no matter how “hard” this may be to others; and the more you do this, more in tune and healthy you become.

And really, there’s nothing to argue, i used to be so stubborn and complain and say “no but this and this happened to me and it’s wrong to be happy w it blablvlablab”. And that’s the other way. Dont fool yourself into thinking that there’s explanation to be bitter because there isn’t; it will only make you sick, and people around you to be sick - i went through this and it’s not worth it. Just be a better person. Just love your journey and stop being mean to your and others. This may bother you - and I hope so - if it does, you’re in the right path!
